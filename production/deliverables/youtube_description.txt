DESCRIPTION (4591 of 5,000 characters)
In 2000, Enron was worth nearly $70 billion. In the fall of 2001 it collapsed in 47 days. This documentary explains, in plain language, how mark-to-market accounting, hidden debt, special-purpose entities and a conflicted CFO opened a gap between the profit Enron reported and the cash it actually had.
